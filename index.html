Questions we get all the time

Frequently asked questions

Still curious? The Help Center has the long answers.

What is an externship?
A remote, part-time project developed with a real company. You work through a company brief over 6 to 11 weeks, on your own schedule, with live sessions, a Program Manager and a cohort of externs. It is project work, not employment. Every finished deliverable goes into your portfolio, stamped “Verified by Extern.”
Do I need prior experience?
No. There is no experience or GPA requirement. Guaranteed acceptance to every externship and project. Learning materials, templates, feedback and milestones are built in.
How much does it cost?
Signing up is free and takes about a minute. Your first project starts with a 7-day free trial. After that you choose the plan that best fits your goals. Paying buys growth and permanence, never access.
How much time does it take?
Externships run 6 to 11 weeks, part-time and fully remote. Live sessions are recorded. Externs regularly finish alongside school or a job.
What do I walk away with?
Real output you can show: a memo, a model, a dashboard, a deck or a prototype, built for a real company. It lives in a shareable portfolio you can send to recruiters, and it stays live even after you leave Extern.
How is this different from a course or a bootcamp?
You are not watching lectures. You are solving an actual challenge the company faces, with the company in the loop, and you finish with work you can put on your resume and talk through in interviews.
