About Extern

A world where anyone can access professional experience as a form of education

At Extern, our mission is to empower everyone to discover and launch their career through meaningful, professional experiences available anytime, anywhere. We pioneered the remote Externship: a flexible, remote project with a company that combines real experience with structured training to help students build confidence and career-ready skills.

Why we exist

Real experience is what stands out.

At Extern, we know today’s job market is tough. Internships are more competitive than ever, and hands-on, real experience is critical to stand out.

Externships were designed to bring value to college students and companies alike. For student members, Extern combines accessible, real-world experiences with expert guidance and a growing community. Companies are able to engage with far greater numbers of students than traditional internships allow, and reach diverse candidates who otherwise would fall outside of their talent funnels.

The case

Why Externships

At Extern, we’re forging a new path for career readiness, one that bridges technical training with hands-on professional experience. Externships offer an inclusive alternative to traditional internships, ensuring that students from all backgrounds can gain work experience that builds their resumes and expands their career potential.

Externships are an innovative way to gain work experience, and a real alternative to the traditional internship. Here’s why they stand out.

Flexible duration

Unlike internships that often last an entire summer or season, Externships are shorter. This allows participants to explore multiple opportunities and career paths within the same timeframe.

Equitable access

Externships aim to provide real experience to everyone, not just the few who land competitive internships. They’re designed to be more inclusive, breaking down barriers to professional development.

Key benefits

Built for students, companies and higher ed.

One Externship serves three sides at once: the student who does the work, the company that briefs it, and the institution that counts it toward career readiness.

The definition

An Externship defined

An Externship is a structured, professional experience guided by mentorship. We created the remote Externship to allow students to collaborate on meaningful projects with companies and build in-demand skills like Generative AI. They develop confidence and advance their careers.
